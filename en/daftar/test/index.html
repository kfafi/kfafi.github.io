How to join the testing group

The test is closed, so we add your email first. Three steps:

  1. Send us the Google account you use on the Play Store — via the “Request to join” button above — so we can add you to the tester list.
  2. Once we confirm you're added, tap the Google Play button above, sign in with that same email, and accept the invitation.
  3. Return to Google Play and install Daftar.

After your email is added and you accept, it can take a few minutes for the app to appear in the store.
